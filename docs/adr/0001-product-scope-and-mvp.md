# ADR 0001: Product scope and MVP vertical slice

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-02
- Authority: Master Plan sections 1–3 and 17

## Decision

Build Bastet Workstation as a local-first personal Agent Team desktop product, distinct from BastetAgentOS. The first vertical slice serves product planners, researchers, and knowledge workers through the exact end-to-end scenario and exclusions defined in the Master Plan. BastetAgentOS handoff remains M9.

## Consequences

Milestones must be implemented in order. M0 establishes repository and specification evidence only and must not claim UI, Pet, adapter, marketplace, deployment, or handoff capabilities.
